Welcome to the ASME KIOT Student Section, a student-driven community at Knowledge Institute of Technology (KIOT) dedicated to bringing engineering learning beyond the classroom. We conduct technical events, workshops, competitions, awareness programmes, and hands-on activities that help students explore mechanical engineering and related technologies.

Our goal is simple — to learn together, build practical skills, and create opportunities for students to grow as engineers and leaders. Explore our journey, discover our events, and be part of the community.

About ASME KIOT Student Section

The ASME KIOT Student Section is a student community established to connect engineering education with practical experience, bridging collegiate theoretical mastery with industrial implementation.

CORE VISION
"To create an active engineering community where students can learn, innovate, collaborate, and grow together."
Our Mission Pillars
Encourage practical learning and technical curiosity across diverse disciplines
Organize meaningful technical and professional industry-vetted programmes
Provide state and national opportunities for students to showcase their engineering skills
Build teamwork, executive leadership, and structured professional communication abilities
Connect students with the wider ASME international engineering community
